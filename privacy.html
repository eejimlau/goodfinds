Privacy

Good Finds MY · last updated 17 November 2026

Who this covers

This page covers the Good Finds MY product page on this site, and the app “NADI Affiliate Intelligence” that the owner of Good Finds MY uses to publish posts to the Good Finds MY Facebook Page and its Instagram account.

What this site collects

Nothing. This site has no sign-in, no forms, no analytics scripts and sets no cookies. It is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub, as the host, may log technical details such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

What the app does

The app is a private tool used only by the owner of Good Finds MY. It publishes the owner’s own posts to the owner’s own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and reads the public figures of those posts (such as reaction and view counts). It does not ask other people to sign in, and it does not collect, store or share personal data about followers, commenters or visitors.

Affiliate links

The links on this site and in Good Finds MY posts are Shopee affiliate links. When you follow one, you go to Shopee, and Shopee handles your visit and any purchase under its own privacy policy. The owner receives only totals from Shopee, such as the number of clicks and the commission earned, and never your name, contact details or what you personally bought.

Deleting data

Because no personal data about visitors or followers is collected, there is nothing held about you to delete. If you believe otherwise, send a message to the Good Finds MY Facebook Page or Instagram account and it will be looked into and removed.

Contact

Message the Good Finds MY Facebook Page or the Instagram account @goodfinds.mystore.
